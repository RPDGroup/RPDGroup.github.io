Our research

From sequences
to dynamic structures.

Our public work brings together molecular simulation and deep learning, spanning conformational ensembles, antibody interface evaluation, and biological sequence optimization.

01 / DYNAMICS

Protein conformational ensembles

Explore protein flexibility through all-atom molecular dynamics, curated datasets, and generative models that support multi-chain systems.

02 / INTERACTIONS

Antibody design & evaluation

Model antigen–antibody interfaces to score binding configurations and screen AI-designed antibody candidates.

03 / SEQUENCES

Sequence modeling & optimization

Apply deep learning and cross-attention models to codon optimization and biological sequence analysis.
